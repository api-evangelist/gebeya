# Gebeya

> Gebeya is an Ethiopian talent-cloud and marketplace technology company headquartered in Addis Ababa, founded in 2016. It trains, vets, and connects African tech talent with organizations, and provides a Marketplace-as-a-Service platform (Gebeya-Jenga) for launching branded, multi-tenant service marketplaces with built-in payments and analytics. Current products include Dala Studio and Jitume, applying LLMs and proprietary data to build AI agents and workforce-intelligence tools for the African digital economy. Gebeya is backed by Partech.
